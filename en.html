FAQ

Frequently asked questions about AEGIFLOW

What is AEGIFLOW?
AEGIFLOW is an engineering firm founded by Ousmane Barry, specialized in custom AI agents and LLMs, API-driven process automation, software development (Web, Mobile and Desktop) and cybersecurity. Every project is built on the Security by Design principle.
Where and in which languages do you work?
We support SMEs, agencies and enterprises in France, Spain, across the European Union and internationally, in English, French and Spanish, remotely or on site.
Is my data sent to public AI models?
No. We design private, isolated AI architectures: models hosted in Europe or on-premise, APIs configured with no retention or training, encryption and role-based access control. Your data stays under your control, in line with the GDPR.
Which tools can you connect and automate?
Virtually any tool with an API, webhooks or a database: CRMs (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive), ERPs (Odoo, SAP), Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, invoicing tools, SQL/NoSQL databases and internal business applications.
Do you also build web, mobile and desktop applications?
Yes. We build web apps and SaaS, client portals, iOS and Android apps and Windows, macOS and Linux desktop software, with AI and your APIs integrated natively and security designed in from the start.
How do I start a project with AEGIFLOW?
Describe your needs through the secure form or message Ousmane Barry directly on LinkedIn. We get back to you within 24 to 48 business hours for a first call and diagnostic.
