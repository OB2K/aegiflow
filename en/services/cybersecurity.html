FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Security by Design?
It is the principle of building security into a system from the design stage instead of patching it afterwards: least privilege, isolation, secrets protection, input validation and offensive testing before delivery.
What is the difference between an audit and a penetration test?
An audit assesses compliance and configuration against standards. A penetration test simulates a real attack to show concretely what an attacker could achieve. Both are complementary.
Can a pentest disrupt my production?
Tests are planned with you and governed by rules of engagement. Risky actions are avoided or performed on a staging environment.
What does the report include?
An executive summary, technical details of each vulnerability with evidence, its risk level and prioritized remediation recommendations.
Do you secure projects you did not build?
Yes. We audit and secure existing applications, APIs, automations and infrastructures, whoever originally built them.
