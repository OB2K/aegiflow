FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a chatbot and an AI agent?
A chatbot answers questions. An AI agent reasons, uses tools and takes actions: reading a document, querying a database, creating a CRM record or sending an email, within defined rules and permissions.
Which language models do you use?
We pick the model based on your needs and data sensitivity: commercial models via API (OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Mistral, Anthropic) configured with no retention, or self-hosted open-source models when data must never leave your infrastructure.
Is my data used to train the AI?
No. Our architectures prevent any reuse of your data to train third-party models. Access is isolated, encrypted and logged.
Can the agent connect to our existing tools?
Yes. Our agents integrate with your CRM, ERP, email, document management, SQL/NoSQL databases and internal APIs, with least-privilege permissions.
How do you measure an AI agent’s quality?
Each agent is evaluated on a set of real cases before going live (accuracy, escalation rate, processing time), then continuously monitored to detect drift.

A process you would like to hand over to an AI agent?

Describe it in a few lines: we will tell you what can be automated, at what security level and with what return on investment.
