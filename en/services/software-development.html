FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which technologies do you use?
We choose the stack for your project: React, Next.js or Vue for web, React Native or Flutter for mobile, Electron or Tauri for desktop, and Node.js, Python or serverless services on the back-end.
Do you build native or cross-platform apps?
Both. Cross-platform reduces cost and time-to-market for most projects; native remains relevant for very specific performance or hardware-access needs.
Who owns the code?
You do. Source code, documentation and access are handed over at delivery, with no hidden lock-in.
Can you take over or modernize existing software?
Yes. We audit the existing system (code, security, performance), then propose a progressive modernization or a rebuild based on the cost/risk balance.
Do you offer maintenance after delivery?
Yes, through ongoing support: security updates, bug fixes, new features and priority support.
