FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you use n8n, Make or custom code?
All three, depending on context. Self-hosted n8n is ideal to keep control of your data, Make for fast-to-build flows, and custom code (Node.js, Python) for high volumes or complex logic.
Can you connect software that has no native integration?
Yes, as long as it exposes an API, a database, exports or webhooks. We then build a custom connector that is documented and secured.
What happens if an API changes or goes down?
Our workflows include error handling, automatic retries and alerts. With ongoing support, we adapt connectors whenever APIs evolve.
Which processes should be automated first?
Repetitive, high-volume and error-prone tasks: data entry, reminders, tool synchronization, document generation and reporting. The Flash Audit identifies and prioritizes them.
Does my data stay in Europe?
We favor self-hosted tools or providers with European hosting, and document every data flow for your GDPR compliance.

How many hours do you lose every week on manual tasks?

Describe your current process: we will identify what can be automated and the expected gain.
